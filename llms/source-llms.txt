# SOURCE Global

> SOURCE Global, PBC (founded as Zero Mass Water) is a Scottsdale, Arizona public
> benefit corporation started in 2014 by Cody Friesen. It built the SOURCE Hydropanel,
> an off-grid solar-thermal device that extracts water vapor from the air and
> mineralizes it into drinking water, deployed in more than 50 countries, and later
> moved into bottled water by acquiring Proud Source Water in 2023. It raised roughly
> USD 270 million, including a USD 130 million Series D in 2022. This file is generated
> by API Evangelist from public sources; it is NOT published by SOURCE Global.

## Product and technology

SOURCE Hydropanel — a self-contained solar-thermal panel that draws water vapor from
ambient air, condenses it, mineralizes it with calcium and magnesium, and stores it in
an onboard reservoir. Rated at roughly five liters (1.3 gallons) of drinking water per
day per panel, operating fully off-grid with sunlight and air as the only inputs.
Product lines spanned residential (Hydropanel R3), commercial and community-scale
arrays, plus SOURCE-branded bottled water and the acquired Proud Source Water brand.
